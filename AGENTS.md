## Mandatory founder rule

> **Preserve the intended effect. Do not literalize the metaphor. Search reality before inventing architecture.**

Research requests containing terms such as morphogenesis, egregore, immune system, self-healing, embodiment, Jarvis, omniscience, or other science-fiction/biological language must be translated into observable target effects.

Then search broadly for mechanically real routes:

- existing UNIIMENTE mechanisms;
- open-source repositories and libraries;
- standards and protocols;
- APIs, tools, plugins, services and models;
- computer-use routes;
- scientific/technical mechanisms from adjacent domains;
- ordinary conventional baselines;
- only then novel mechanisms/architecture where a residual CapabilityDeficit remains.

Extract mechanism cards rather than product hype: primitive operation, state, transitions, authority, information, resource flow, feedback, proof, failure and recovery.

Canonical morphogenesis is functional capability formation/reconfiguration in response to verified discrepancy. Biological/developmental systems are sources of possible mechanisms, not mandatory implementation form.

## Evidence

Preserve source provenance, dates, licenses, maintenance status, counterevidence, uncertainty and negative results. Do not claim that a discovered paper/repository/tool proves UNIIMENTE possesses a capability until it is actually integrated, tested and verified at the appropriate tier.

## Authority

Research may recommend; it does not authorize execution or capability promotion. New tools or generated artifacts receive no inherited authority.
